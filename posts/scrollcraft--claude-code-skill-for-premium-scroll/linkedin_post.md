🛠️ scrollcraft is a Claude Code skill that treats interaction and design craft as one unified job, not two separate phases that produce forgettable pages or flashy messes.

What makes it different:

✅ **Eight mutually exclusive page grammars** — filmic one-shot, continuous world, typographic poster, split stage, gallery. Pick one and it forbids what the others require, so two builds cannot quietly become the same site.

✅ **A fingerprint gate** — new builds must differ from your previous pages on at least 4 of 6 dimensions: grammar, nav, hero, act shape, close, signature move. Fail the gate and you change the plan, not the registry.

✅ **Automated verification pass** — a headless browser walks your finished page at every scroll position and reports dead scroll, faded copy that never reaches full opacity, and real contrast measured on the composited page per line.

It is opinionated on purpose. It will refuse the layouts and palettes that make AI pages recognizable, and it will argue with you about your peak.
