🧹 Does your AI-built UI look a little... slopped?

You know the look: purple gradients everywhere, glows on every card, emoji instead of icons, copy that shouts, spacing that's off.

unslop is a Claude Code skill that fixes exactly that, step by step:

✅ Audits first: lists every sign of slop and where it lives
✅ Proposes a direction, a character and a signature detail from your product before touching anything
✅ Fixes in passes: spacing, color, icons, copy, type, layout, details, visuals, motion
✅ Works with Next.js, React, Vue, Svelte, Tailwind, plain HTML/CSS, or a live site from a link
✅ If the screen is already good, it says so instead of redesigning for the sake of it
✅ Can generate hero scenes and even a short video of your screen unslopping itself
