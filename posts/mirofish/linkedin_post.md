🐟 What if you could run a what-if scenario with thousands of AI agents instead of just your own head?

MiroFish is an open-source prediction engine that turns seed material into a simulated world you can question and explore.

✅ Upload news, a policy draft, a data report, or even a story as your seed material
✅ Builds a graph and sets up agent personas and relationships
✅ Runs simulations where agents update their memory as events unfold
✅ Generates a prediction report, and you can chat directly with individual agents or the ReportAgent
✅ Demo includes simulating the lost ending of Dream of the Red Chamber from its first 80 chapters

Worth noting: these are hypothetical simulation outputs, not guaranteed predictions, and setup requires your own OpenAI-compatible LLM API key plus a Zep Cloud API key, so usage isn't free.
