🎙️ Why are you still paying monthly for AI voice cloning?

Tools like ElevenLabs lock your voice pipeline behind a cloud API and a recurring bill, plus your audio leaves your machine every time.

VoiceStudio is a free, fully-local, open-source alternative that runs entirely on your own device:

✅ Voice cloning from a clean reference recording
✅ Voice design: describe a voice in words and generate it
✅ Video dubbing with speech timed to the original
✅ Dictation widget and local transcription
✅ Audiobook and batch narration in 646 languages
✅ Local API, MCP, recipes and watch folders for automation
✅ Works on macOS, Windows, Linux, Docker, Android and iOS

Nearly 97K GitHub stars, AGPL-3.0 licensed, no paid cloud behind it. Default engine powered by k2-fsa/OmniVoice, with dozens more in the Model Manager. CPU works (slower), with CUDA, Metal, ROCm and Vulkan support.

Install: curl -fsSL https://voicestudio.sh/install | sh
