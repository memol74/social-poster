🔍 Are you sure your AI agent does what it's supposed to do?

Most teams ship an agent, watch it work a few times, and assume it's fine. Then it hits a request outside its role and nobody finds out until a customer does.

iFixAi is an open-source tool for independent auditing of AI agents.

✅ Puts your agent inside a simulation of your business
✅ Sends it a full day of requests, including ones that push past its role
✅ A judge from a different provider grades every reply (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, Azure, Bedrock, OpenRouter and more)
✅ Every finding shows exactly what was asked and what the agent did
✅ 10 default inspections combining red teaming, governance, assurance and philosophy
✅ Works with any agent with a chat endpoint, plugs into Claude Code and Codex

Apache-2.0 licensed, 41,403+ GitHub stars, 1,766 forks.

Quick start:
pip install ifixai
