🚀 What if you never paid a PaaS bill again?

Vercel, Heroku, and Netlify are great until the invoice shows up.

Coolify is a free, open-source, self-hostable alternative that runs on servers you already control:

✅ Deploys apps, databases, and services on your own VPS, bare metal, or even a Raspberry Pi
✅ Builds from GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Gitea
✅ Automatic deployments on Git push, with pull-request previews
✅ Automatic HTTPS and one-click rollbacks
✅ 360+ one-click services, persistent storage, generated credentials, and backups

65,015 GitHub stars. 5,835 forks. Apache-2.0 licensed. Free forever.

Install it with one command:
curl -fsSL https://cdn.coollabs.io/coolify/install.sh | bash
