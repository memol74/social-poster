🛠️ What if you could rebuild any website, app or game, just by pointing at it?

Most of us study other people's products by guessing from the outside, then rebuilding blind.

Craft Anything flips that: real tools inspect the target first, then an AI agent builds your version.

✅ Study: real inspection tools examine how the website, app or game actually works
✅ Build: a coding agent (Claude Code first, GitHub Copilot CLI also supported) builds a new project in the stack you choose
✅ Check: compares your build against the original, websites checked at three screen widths
✅ Ready: refine until it matches
✅ 320 tools, each both a CLI command and an MCP tool, no AI required just to inspect
✅ Clean-room engine, 100% Rust, runs on macOS, Windows, Linux, FreeBSD and Web

17,807 stars and 3,257 forks, 6 days after creation. Apache-2.0, early alpha.
