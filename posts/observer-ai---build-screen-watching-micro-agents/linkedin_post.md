🚨 The usual approach is fragile:
• Pixel-based checks break when UIs change

Observer AI solves this with micro-agents that use local vision models to watch your screen, camera, microphone, or clipboard.

1️⃣ Vision models check your screen in a loop and trigger code when they spot what you specified
2️⃣ Built-in notification tools send Discord, Telegram, WhatsApp, email, or SMS alerts instantly
3️⃣ Smart video recording with custom labels captures critical moments automatically

The repo has over 2,000 stars ⭐ and runs entirely locally with bundled llama.cpp or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
