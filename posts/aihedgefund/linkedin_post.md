📈 What if you could run a hedge fund without risking a single real dollar?

Most people wanting to test trading ideas either risk real capital or build a backtesting setup from scratch.

AI Hedge Fund is an open-source proof of concept that lets you do neither:

✅ LLM investor persona agents reason over point-in-time fundamentals and emit trading signals
✅ Paper trading mode: a ledger of real market days, fake money, an approval step showing the exact decision before anything trades, and a kill switch
✅ Every session appended to a hash-chained ledger
✅ Backtesting mode: replay a fund over history and watch its equity curve race the benchmark
✅ Needs a Financial Datasets API key plus one model API key (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google, xAI, Kimi, or TypeSafe)

64,397 stars. MIT licensed. Educational and research use only, it does not execute real trades.

Install: pipx install aihf, then run aihf
