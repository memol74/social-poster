🤝 What if your AI sales rep ran on a subscription you already pay for?

Most AI sales tools bill you per API token on top of your other subscriptions, and most of them send emails without asking you first.

Harvey is an open-source autonomous sales agent that runs on your Claude Pro/Max plan via Claude Code, or your ChatGPT plan via Codex:

✅ Finds businesses and reads their websites before writing anything
✅ Writes cold emails that reference something specific about each prospect
✅ Sends campaigns, reads replies, and works each conversation toward a meeting
✅ Runs a heartbeat loop every 15 minutes: wake, quiet hours, budget check, decide, act, log, sleep
✅ Every outgoing email waits for your approval in the Outbox: approve, edit, or reject
✅ Local dashboard at localhost:5555, token-locked, data stored in SQLite
✅ Works with gmail, microsoft, smtp, or instantly as mail providers

3,702 stars, 764 forks, MIT licensed. No API key, no per-token billing, subject to your subscription's usage limits.

Install and run: harvey run
